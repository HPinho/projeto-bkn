# Third-party assets

## Google Sans Flex

The Baken OS build uses Google Sans Flex to generate a local alpha atlas.
Google Sans Flex is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The complete
license is included in `assets/fonts/Google_Sans_Flex.zip`.

Source: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Google+Sans+Flex

## Morphicons

The media controls use selected SVG states from the local Morphicons/Lottie
packages. Only the states required by the shell are compiled into the EFI
atlas. Morphicons is licensed under the MIT License; the complete upstream
license is included in `assets/icons/morphicons-main.zip`.

Source: https://www.morphicons.com/

## Ionicons

The Baken OS application and system icon atlases use selected filled Ionicons
SVG assets. Ionicons is licensed under the MIT License. The local source
package is `assets/icons/ionicons.designerpack`.

Source: https://github.com/ionic-team/ionicons/blob/main/LICENSE
